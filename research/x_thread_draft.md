# X thread draft (English) – review before posting

1/ We analysed 16,683 trades on @JupiterExchange Predict to answer one question: are the leaderboard's top traders skilled, or lucky? 🧵

2/ Short answer: the leaderboard measures luck. Across 125 top wallets, P&L and measured edge (closing-line value) had a correlation of ~0.02. Only 2 wallets showed a positive edge — about what chance alone produces.

3/ What IS measurable: cost. One entry on Jupiter Predict costs ~4.6% vs the mid price on average ($66.7k on $1.44M of trades we could measure). Live trading: ~4.9%. Contracts under 20¢: ~11.7%.

4/ Where does it go? Jupiter passes on Polymarket's exact price (we matched 537 fills to their Polymarket executions, no markup). The cost is Polymarket's spread + Jupiter's fee, roughly half and half.

5/ We also tested — with the protocol written down before running — whether Jupiter flow is "dumb money", whether routing between Polymarket and Kalshi saves money, and whether order flow (absorption, delta) predicts prices. None held up. We're publishing those too.

6/ So instead of another alpha tool, we're building EDGEHOG: see the real cost of a trade in dollars before you sign it, and an execution report + replay after. Built on Solana for the Perps & Prediction Markets hackathon.

7/ Methods, numbers and limits: <link to research page>. Feedback from Jupiter Predict traders very welcome — what would you want to see about your own trades?
